Single Circle Noise Test (October 14, 2026)

Completed single-circle study · 1,440 paired cases · 4,320 reconstructions

1. Inverse problems and protocol

  • CT: 10 equally spaced parallel-beam projection angles over \([0,\pi)\).
  • MRI: single-coil Fourier measurements with variable-density Cartesian sampling at nominal \(4\times\) acceleration.
  • Noise: 1/5/10/20%.
  • Resolutions: \(64^2,96^2,128^2,160^2,200^2,250^2\).
  • Samples: 30 per resolution, inverse problem and noise level; paired across methods.
  • Models: non-attention flow-matching UNets.
  • Euler steps: 5.
  • Optimizer: LBFGS.
  • Learning rate: 1.
  • DLR rank: \(r=8\).
  • Regularization: none.

2. Best, median and worst relative image error

Image error (%): \(100\|\hat{x}-x_{\mathrm{GT}}\|_2/\|x_{\mathrm{GT}}\|_2\). Each row summarizes 30 final reconstructions at one resolution and noise level. Best/median/worst are computed separately for each method. Within each row, green marks the lowest best error, grey the lowest median, and red the highest worst error.
